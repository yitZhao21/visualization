## Assignment 2: Good and Bad Data Visualization

### Requirements:

- Data visualizations are important tools for communication and convincing; we need to be able to evaluate the ways that data are presented in visual form to be critical consumers of information 
- To test your evaluation skills, locate two public data visualizations online, one good and one bad  
    - You can find data visualizations at https://public.tableau.com/app/discover or https://datavizproject.com/, or anywhere else you like! 
- For each visualization (good and bad):  
    - Explain (with reference to material covered up to date, along with readings and other scholarly sources, as needed) why you classified that visualization the way you did.
      ```
      Good visualization: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/bo.mccready8742/viz/TwoErasofSafetyIronViz2025Winner/TwoEras

      This visualisation is very clear in storytelling through dividing the timeline into pre- and post-Flight 1549 (Year 2009). Colorcoding is very helpful for identifying the time periods. The visualisation leads with a real-life event. It is also very selective with information by selecting the 5 airports with consistent easily comparable mini-graphs.

      The plots are also very informative demonstrating both the freqeuncy of strikes and how much damage it caused.

      Users are able to click at each bar or each specific airport plot to explore additional information.


      Bad visualisation:https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/lilla.rasztik/viz/2025HabitTracker/HabitTracker

      This visualization is bad because:
      (1) The black/gray color palletes are not very visually appealing for the story/theme, which is about building a strong body. "Didn't do" and "not tracked are black vs. gray cells, which are hard to differentiate.
      (2) It is unclear which activity is associated with which parts of the center circle, because the legends are not structured intuitively. 
      (3) When the cursor is hovered above the center circle, the specific date will pop up, but the area for each date is too narrow. The pop-up only includes the specific date but no additional information related to whether the area is related to a specific activity or whether this activity is done that particular day.






      ```
    - How could this data visualization have been improved?  
      ```
      For the good visualisation: 
      There could be a Y-Axis on the bar chart in the center. There can also be a legend for the airport charts. The units of the frequency/damage can also be specified to help with quantifying. “High damage" seems to be subjectively classified.
      The airport plots' font can be larger. The story is a lot of text, even though emotionally appealing for storytelling.
      
      For the bad visualisation: 
      (1) Can use lighter colors or higher contrast to ease reading, especially to distinguish between "Done" and "Didn't do".
      (2) The center circle is unclear in its timeline (i.e., currently each small slice represents a day, but it is not straightforward at first glance)
      (3) May be easier if providing a bar chart that compares the completion rate of each day across time. These info are currently hidden under the "monthly success rate" icon.
      (4) Can add  legend explaining which activity is associted with which ring (inner, center, outer, etc.)
      (5) May be helpful to categorize the activities as well, some are more physical while some are more mental-health-oriented.
      
      ```
